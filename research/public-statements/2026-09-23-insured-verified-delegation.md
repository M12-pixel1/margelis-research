# Insured Verified Delegation — Public Category Statement

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Organisation:** Rūpestėlis Holding / Margelis.ai  
**Status:** Public category statement; non-enabling technical disclosure.

## The question

As AI agents move from generating information to performing economic actions on behalf of people and organisations, a new risk question emerges:

> **Who bears the loss when an AI agent was legitimately authorised to spend money, but executed the wrong economic action?**

This case is distinct from a purely unauthorised payment or a conventional cyber incident.

An agent may be correctly identified and operate within a bounded financial authority while still making an incorrect execution decision.

## Proposed category

We use the working term **Insured Verified Delegation** for the intersection of:

- bounded delegated authority;
- controlled AI-agent execution;
- independent outcome verification;
- evidence suitable for third-party review; and
- potential insurance underwriting of resulting economic loss.

The central principle is:

> **Intelligence is not authority.**

A model's ability to recommend or prepare an action should be separated from the authority to execute that action.

## Initial validation question

A practical first test is intentionally narrow:

Can an independent evaluator determine, after a bounded AI-agent economic action:

1. who delegated authority;
2. what authority was granted;
3. whether the requested action remained within that authority;
4. what actually executed;
5. what outcome was independently observed; and
6. what verified financial loss, if any, resulted?

This work is being explored through technical validation, insurance-underwriting discussion, and European stakeholder engagement.

## Scope boundary

This statement does **not** disclose the internal enforcement implementation, policy logic, scoring methods, runtime control architecture, claim algorithm, or other potentially protectable technical mechanisms.

It is not a claim of regulatory approval, certification, independent validation, patent protection, or a finished insurance product.

The purpose of this statement is to identify and timestamp the category-level problem and research direction.
